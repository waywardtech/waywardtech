# Daniel Teixeira-Gomes

Technologist, systems architect and filmmaker in Fort Collins, Colorado. Principal of [The Far Edge](https://thefaredge.com).

For more than three decades I've built where complex systems meet the people who use them:

- **American Express Europe:** DB2 billing migration and digital card and retail activation
- **IBM for Telstra and Pacific Bell:** billing, retail and network middleware for one of the first fully digital mobile networks
- **Xenium:** pre-Google natural-language search (192.com)
- **San Francisco:** nonprofit, education and public-media infrastructure
- **Electronic Arts:** launch content

This account is where I build now: small, complete, self-contained things. Embedded displays, WebXR worlds, home-lab tools, and apps for my family.

## How I build

- **Finish things.** Every repo here runs, and its README tells you how.
- **Self-contained over clever.** No build step where one isn't needed.
- **Document for the next person,** including the troubleshooting table I wish I'd had.
